# Part 34 (ج2/ص166 – ج2/ص183)

This part is eschatology: the gathering, standing for judgement, the reckoning (الحساب), entering without reckoning, and the record books (الصحف). It has no direct discussion of the divine attributes. It never uses the terms تفويض, إثبات المعنى, بلا كيف, أمروها, etc. Below are the only marginal items that touch on attributes, all of them indirect.

## Evidence
1. [ج2/ص172] speaker: author (listing views; third view not rejected)
   kind: other (marginal; divine speech / كلام)
   quote: «(الثالث) : أن يكلم الله عباده في شأن أعمالهم، وكيفية ما لها من الثواب، وما عليها من العقاب، وفي هذا من صحيح الأخبار وصريح الآثار ما يقلع شرور من في قلبه نوع اختلاج أصل كل شبهة وبدعة»
   note: Of three views on what the reckoning means, he backs the one where God actually speaks to His servants with "sound reports" and presents doubt about it as bid'a. This implicitly takes divine speech literally. The first view ("God creates necessary knowledge in their hearts") is only reported.

2. [ج2/ص175] speaker: quoting Ibn Taymiyya, al-Wasitiyya (endorsed; brought in to settle the question of reckoning the kuffar)
   kind: other (marginal; takes the texts as they describe)
   quote: «يحاسب الله تعالى الخلق، ويخلو بعبده المؤمن، ويقرره بذنوبه كما وصف ذلك في الكتاب والسنة»
   note: Divine acts (being alone with the believer, making him acknowledge his sins) are affirmed "as described in Book and Sunna". He gives no commentary on the attribute itself.

3. [ج2/ص177] speaker: quoting al-Qurtubi «كغيره» (reported approvingly as a فائدة)
   kind: other (marginal; divine speech without an intermediary)
   quote: «إن الله تعالى يكلم المسلمين عند الحساب من غير ترجمان إكراما لهم، ولا يكلم الكافرين»
   note: Direct divine speech is affirmed as real, backed by the hadith «ثلاثة لا يكلمهم الله». There is no discussion of meaning or manner.

4. [ج2/ص178] speaker: author (lexical gloss on a hadith wording)
   kind: other (marginal)
   quote: «وثلاثة حثيات من حثيات ربي ... ويروى " حفنات " بالفتح، وهو الغرف ملء اليدين، وقيل الحثية باليد، والحفنة باليدين.»
   note: He glosses the word حثية / حفنة literally ("a scoop with the two hands") in a hadith that attributes it to "my Lord". He neither applies it to the divine hands nor interprets it away (no ta'wil, no tafwid). This is weak, incidental evidence.

5. [ج2/ص173] speaker: author
   kind: other (not about divine attributes)
   quote: «كيفيات الحساب مختلفة، وأحوالها متباينة»
   note: He uses the word كيفية here only for the ways the reckoning is carried out, not for an attribute. It is noted only so the term's use is on record.

## Strongest item in this part
Item 1. It is the only place where the author himself takes a side: he prefers God's real speech to His servants and calls doubt about it a شبهة/بدعة. Even so, it is about divine speech within eschatology, not about meaning versus how, so it is weak for the team's question.
