# Part 33 (ج2/ص149 – ج2/ص165)

## Evidence
1. [ج2/ص151] speaker: author
   kind: tafwid-kayf-only (ambiguous; could also be read as tafwid-ma'na)
   quote: «(قوله) تقذرهم نفس الله هو من المتشابه والإيمان به واجب كما أخبر لا كما يتوهمه البشر.»
   note: A short gloss on the hadith of Ibn 'Amr (the fire gathering the worst of people). He classes «نفس الله» as متشابه and tells the reader to believe in it "as He reported, not as humans imagine". He gives no meaning, but he does not deny one either. It reads as a rejection of tashbih rather than an explicit consignment of meaning. It matters for the question because he calls an attribute-text «متشابه».

2. [ج2/ص158] speaker: author, endorsing quotations from al-Jalal al-Dawani and al-Razi
   kind: against-ta'wil (eschatology, not the attributes)
   quote: «وهذا نص صريح في الحشر الجسماني يقلع عرق التأويل بالكلية.» / al-Razi: «ورد في عدة مواضع من القرآن المجيد التصريح به بحيث لا يقبل التأويل أصلا. انتهى.»
   note: This rejects the philosophers' ta'wil of bodily resurrection: a clear text binds to its plain meaning. It only shows his general view of ta'wil and says nothing directly about the attributes.

3. [ج2/ص164] speaker: author, quoting hadiths (reported, no comment)
   kind: other
   quote: «يقبض الله الأرض يوم القيامة، ويطوي السماء بيمينه ثم يقول أنا الملك» / «يطوي الله السماوات يوم القيامة ثم يأخذهن بيده اليمنى»
   note: He cites the hadiths on يمين and يد اليمنى with no gloss, ta'wil or tafwid remark. He simply passes on the wording.

4. [ج2/ص165] speaker: author, reporting views on Abu Hurayra's «أبيت»
   kind: other (marginal)
   quote: «وقيل: إن سر ذلك لا يعلمه إلا الله؛ لأنه من أسرار الربوبية.»
   note: This is about the time between the two blasts of the trumpet, not about an attribute. It is listed only because it uses the wording of consigning knowledge to God.

## Strongest item in this part
Item 1. It is his only comment on an attribute in this part. He calls «نفس الله» متشابه and says to believe in it «كما أخبر لا كما يتوهمه البشر». The wording rules out tashbih but does not settle whether he consigns the meaning or only the how.
