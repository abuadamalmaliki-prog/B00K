# Part 45 (ج2/ص364 – ج2/ص381)

Context: this whole part is the section on the ranking of the Companions (أهل بدر، أهل الشجرة، أهل أحد، عائشة وخديجة، فصل في فضل الصحابة). It contains no discussion of the divine attributes as a theological topic. The items below are weak and only touch on the question in passing.

## Evidence
1. [ج2/ص372] speaker: author
   kind: explains-meaning (weak)
   quote: «فوصفهم في الموضعين بالعفو، ووصف أهل البيعة بالرضى، وهو أعلى وأسنى وأفضل من العفو، وهذا ظاهر»
   note: To rank the people of the Pledge above the people of Uhud, he treats God's رضى and عفو as two distinct divine acts/attributes, with رضى being the higher one. This assumes each has its own understood content (it is not consigned meaning). His argument is about ranking the Companions, though, not about the attributes.

2. [ج2/ص366] speaker: author (paraphrasing/glossing Umar)
   kind: other (weak)
   quote: «كانت رحمة من الله - يعني إخفاءها - ... أو كانت موضع رحمة من الله، ومحل رضوانه لإنزاله الرضى على المؤمنين عندها»
   note: A passing gloss on the tree of the Pledge. He speaks of God "sending down" رضى on the believers there, taking the wording at face value. There is no theological discussion.

3. [ج2/ص377] speaker: author
   kind: defines-terms (marginal, not about the attributes)
   quote: «والكف عما جرى بينهم مما لعله لم يصح عنهم، وما صح فله تأويلات سائغة»
   note: He uses تأويل in a positive sense ("acceptable interpretations") for the Companions' conflicts. This shows only that the word is not always negative in his usage, and has no bearing on the attributes.

4. [ج2/ص380] speaker: quoting Ibn Mas'ud (via Abu Dawud al-Tayalisi; reported approvingly, as evidence)
   kind: other (weak)
   quote: «إن الله نظر في قلوب العباد، فنظر قلب محمد خير قلوب العباد، فبعثه برسالته»
   note: A report cited for the merit of the Companions. It speaks of God's "looking" literally, but he makes no comment on its meaning.

(Hadiths that mention اطلاع الله على أهل بدر [ص364] and الإيحاء/الكلام [ص367–368] are only cited for the virtues of the Companions, and he comments on them only in fiqh/hadith terms. The «اعملوا ما شئتم» discussion on ص364–365 deals with how to read ظاهر in a command form, not with the attributes.)

## Strongest item in this part
Item 1. It is the only place where the author gives divine attributes (رضى vs عفو) distinct, ranked content. It is still weak, since he is ranking the Companions, not discussing the attributes.
