# Part 47 (ج2/ص400 – ج2/ص417)

Context: this part is wholly on تفضيل صالحي البشر على الملائكة (تنبيهات 1–5), then al-Qarafi's twenty rules of التفضيل (via al-ʿIzz b. ʿAbd al-Salam). Nothing in it discusses تفويض, تأويل or the meaning of the khabari attributes directly. There are only incidental remarks, listed below. All are weak or indirect.

## Evidence
1. [ج2/ص413] speaker: author (correcting quoted al-Qarafi)
   kind: other (ithbat of كلام on the Salafi/Hanbali line, against the Ashʿari view)
   quote: «قلت: وهذا على حسب اعتقاده من أنها مخلوقة، وليست هي من كلام رب العالمين، والحق أن ما بين دفتي المصحف كلام رب العالمين وحبله المتين»
   note: al-Qarafi had said the letters of the mushaf are honoured only because they point to (دالة على) Allah's speech. The author rejects this as the view that the letters are created and affirms that what is in the mushaf is itself Allah's speech. This is a doctrinal ithbat with positive content, but it is about كلام, not about تفويض.

2. [ج2/ص408] speaker: quoting an unnamed source (the argument appears to come from al-Razi's tafsir), apparently endorsed since it sits in the author's reply to the Muʿtazila
   kind: explains-meaning (incidental)
   quote: «(الثاني) أنه قال: ومن عنده وهذه عندية الفضيلة والقربة ... وقال في الملائكة إنهم عند ربهم، وقال في وصف المنكسرة قلوبهم إن ربهم عندهم»
   note: «من عنده» (the angels being "with Him") is read in the opponents' argument as nearness of rank and honour. The reply sets it against «أنا عند المنكسرة قلوبهم». Neither side gives it a spatial or kayf reading. This is passing exegesis, not a statement of method.

3. [ج2/ص403] speaker: quoting al-Bayhaqi (reported; al-Bayhaqi himself says «وفي ثبوته نظر»)
   kind: other
   quote: «لا أجعل من خلقته بيدي ونفخت فيه من روحي كمن قلت له: كن فكان»
   note: the hadith of the hand is cited as a proof for preferring humans to angels. The argument rests on Adam being created by the hand as a special honour, distinct from creation by «كن». No comment on the meaning of اليد.

4. [ج2/ص412، ص413، ص415] speaker: quoting al-Qarafi (reported, no objection except item 1)
   kind: other
   quote: «الخامسة التفضيل لشرف الموصوف، منها صفات الله تعالى من علمه وكلامه وقدراته وإرادته ... أفضل من غيرها»; «كتفضيل علم الله تعالى على قدرته وإرادته وسمعه وبصره، لتعلقه بجميع الواجبات والممكنات والمستحيلات»; «وليس في صفات الله السبعة التي تثبتها الصفاتية إلا القدرة والإرادة فقط»
   note: Ashʿari-style talk of the seven attributes and their تعلق, quoted without comment. It treats these attributes as real attributes with distinct content, but it is al-Qarafi's framework, not the author's.

5. [ج2/ص417] speaker: quoting al-ʿIzz b. ʿAbd al-Salam (reported)
   kind: other
   quote: «كمعرفة وجود الإله وصفاته الذاتية والسلبية والفعلية ... والأنس بقربه وجواره وسماع كلامه وسلامه ... والنظر إلى وجه الله الكريم»
   note: speaks of knowing the attributes and of the ruʾya (seeing وجه الله, hearing His speech) as the highest blessing. It is incidental and not discussed.

## Strongest item in this part
1. It is the only place in the part where the author speaks in his own voice on an attribute. He corrects an Ashʿari reading and affirms that the mushaf is Allah's actual speech. It bears on the question only indirectly. The part has no direct evidence for either إثبات المعنى or تفويض المعنى.

## Coverage
Read lines 1–110 of 110 (reached the end). Lines 1–5 and 107–110 are overlap and were not reported.
