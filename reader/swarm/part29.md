# Part 29 (ج2/ص77 – ج2/ص95)

## Evidence
لا شيء

Nothing in this part bears on the divine attributes. The pages cover eschatology only: signs of the Mahdi, the Sufyani and the fitan before him, the Mahdi's bay'a and reign, the Kaysaniyya, the Dajjal (his description, his paradise and fire, the man he kills and revives), and the descent of 'Isa (proof from Quran, Sunna and ijma', his appearance and conduct).

Marginal note on terms only, not evidence on the question:
- [ج2/ص92] quoting Ibn Hibban and others, then al-Barzanji (al-Isha'a) and Shaykh Mar'i, who prefer this view (reported and apparently endorsed, since he gives no rebuttal): the Dajjal's paradise and fire are «على طريق التخييل لا الحقيقة». Against them: «وقال جماعة منهم ابن العربي: بل هي على ظاهرها». Then «والتحقيق الأول». This shows the author using ظاهر/حقيقة in the ordinary sense (real vs. apparent). It has nothing to do with the sifat.
- [ج2/ص94] author: the men the Dajjal kills and revives are devils, «في رأي العين لا على الحقيقة». Same ordinary use of حقيقة.
