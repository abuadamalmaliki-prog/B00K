# Part 41 (ج2/ص291 – ج2/ص307)

Subject matter of this part: miracles (the Qur'an, the splitting of the moon), the superiority of the Prophet ﷺ, the أولو العزم, رسل and أنبياء, refutation of the claim that ولاية is above نبوة, and the عصمة of the prophets (from كفر, إفك, خيانة). Nothing here directly addresses the divine attributes or the question of تفويض. Only the marginal items below come close.

## Evidence
1. [ج2/ص295] speaker: author
   kind: other
   quote: «وفي شرعه إنما تنعقد الأيمان بأسماء الله تعالى وصفاته وكلامه لا بدون ذلك»
   note: In passing, he treats صفات and كلام as real things one can swear by, set apart from created things. This is in the discussion of Allah swearing by the Prophet's life. The link to the question is weak.
2. [ج2/ص300] speaker: quoting بعض العلماء (reported, not challenged)
   kind: explains-meaning (marginal)
   quote: «لعل تقديم موسى ﵇ لأنه كلمه الله ثم عيسى ﵇ لأنه كلمة الله»
   note: Allah's speaking to Moses is taken at face value as a distinguishing merit. This is incidental, in the ranking of the أولو العزم.
3. [ج2/ص302] speaker: quoting Ibn Taymiyya (endorsed; he praises the refutation)
   kind: other
   quote: «جعلوا ما يلقى في نفوسهم ... أعظم من تكليم موسى بن عمران ﵇ قال: وهي في الحقيقة إيحاءات شيطانية»
   note: تكليم موسى is treated as a real divine speaking, in the course of refuting the Sufi claim that ولاية is above نبوة. Incidental.
4. [ج2/ص298] speaker: author
   kind: defines-terms (marginal)
   quote: «ومراعاة علو تلك المراتب التي لا تدرك كنه حقائقها أكثر العقول»
   note: He uses «كنه حقائقها» for the inner reality that minds cannot grasp. Here it refers to the ranks of the prophets, not to divine attributes. It may be useful only as a sample of how he uses كنه.
5. [ج2/ص306] speaker: quoting Imam Ahmad via Ibn Rajab (endorsed)
   kind: other
   quote: «احذروا الكلام، فإن أصحاب الكلام لا يئول أمرهم إلى خير»
   note: A general warning against علم الكلام, given in the context of whether the Prophet was ever on his people's religion. It says nothing about attributes.

## Strongest item in this part
None. The part has no substantive evidence on إثبات المعنى or تفويض المعنى. Item 1 is the closest, and it is only incidental.
