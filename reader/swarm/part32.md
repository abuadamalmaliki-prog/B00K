# Part 32 (ج2/ص131 – ج2/ص148)

This part sits in the signs of the Hour (أشراط الساعة): the end of the smoke (الدخان), the seventh sign (رفع القرآن), the eighth (طلوع الشمس من مغربها) and the ninth (دابة الأرض). There is no discussion of the divine attributes as such. Only marginal items follow.

## Evidence
1. [ج2/ص132] speaker: author, quoting Ibn Taymiyya as reporting the Salaf (endorsed; he points back to his earlier section on Kalam)
   kind: other (explains-meaning, attribute of speech)
   quote: «ما حكاه شيخ الإسلام ابن تيمية قدس الله روحه عن السلف من أن القرآن العظيم كلام الله منزل غير مخلوق منه بدأ وإليه يعود، وأن معنى وإليه يعود ما جاء في الآثار أن القرآن يسرى به حتى لا يبقى في المصاحف منه حرف ولا في القلوب منه آية.»
   note: He gives a concrete meaning for a credal formula about divine speech instead of leaving it unexplained. It is about the creed formula, not a ṣifa text, so it is only weak evidence.

2. [ج2/ص132] speaker: author citing a hadith (from al-Daylami), merely reported
   kind: other
   quote: «حتى يرجع القرآن من حيث جاء له دوي حول العرش كدوي النحل فيقول الله ﷿ ما لك؟ فيقول منك خرجت وإليك أعود»
   note: He reports it in support of the sign without any comment on attributes. It is relevant only as background to «منه بدأ».

3. [ج2/ص133] speaker: author citing an athar of Salman (via Abu al-Shaykh), merely reported
   kind: other
   quote: «إني أنا الله لا إله إلا أنا رضاي كلام وغضبي كلام ورحمتي كلام وعذابي كلام»
   note: He passes on the athar with no comment about رضا or غضب as attributes. It is not usable evidence for either position.

No passage in this part addresses تفويض, تأويل, معنى/كيفية, or the مفوضة.

## Strongest item in this part
Item 1, and it is weak. Endorsing Ibn Taymiyya, he explains what «وإليه يعود» means in the doctrine of divine speech, which leans slightly toward giving content rather than consigning meaning. It is not about the ṣifāt texts directly.
