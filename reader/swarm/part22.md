# Part 22 (ج1/ص399 – ج1/ص416)

Context: this part ends the section on whether the repentance of the زنديق, the داعية بدعة and the killer is accepted (ص399–403), then opens the فصل on الإيمان: it is قول وقصد وعمل, and it increases and decreases (ص403–416). Nothing here deals directly with the divine attributes. The items below are indirect, mainly in Ibn Taymiyya's "ways in which faith increases", which al-Saffarini quotes at length and endorses.

## Evidence
1. [ج1/ص414] speaker: quoting Ibn Taymiyya (endorsed; quoted as authoritative inside the author's own argument that faith increases)
   kind: ithbat-ma'na
   quote: «فكذلك معرفة القلب وتصديقه يتفاضل أعظم من ذلك من وجوه متعددة للمعاني التي يؤمن بها من معاني أسماء الله - تعالى - وكلامه، ويتفاضل الناس في معرفتها أعظم من تفاضلهم في معرفة غيرها»
   note: The third way faith increases. It treats the meanings of God's names as things the believer knows and believes, and says people differ in how well they know them. That assumes the meanings can be known, which is not تفويض المعنى.

2. [ج1/ص414] speaker: quoting Ibn Taymiyya (endorsed)
   kind: ithbat-ma'na
   quote: «وكذلك من عرف أسماء الله - تعالى - وصفاته وآياته كان إيمانه أكمل»
   note: The second way faith increases. Knowing the names and attributes in detail makes faith more complete. This implies the attributes have content that can be known.

3. [ج1/ص412] speaker: quoting Ibn Taymiyya (endorsed)
   kind: ithbat-ma'na (general, about the Qur'an)
   quote: «إذا تليت عليه الآيات ازداد قلبه بفهم القرآن ومعرفة معانيه من علم الإيمان ... فيزداد علمه بالله»
   note: Faith grows by understanding the meanings of the Qur'an, and this adds to knowledge of God. It is about the Qur'an's meanings in general, not the attribute verses specifically.

4. [ج1/ص415–416] speaker: quoting Ibn Taymiyya (endorsed)
   kind: other (supports ithbat-ma'na weakly)
   quote: «ثم يسمع الآية، والحديث، أو يتدبر ذلك، أو يفسر له معناه ... فيصدق بما كان مكذبا به ... وهذا تصديق جديد وإيمان جديد»
   note: The eighth way. Having a text's meaning explained (تفسير المعنى) is a normal route to new faith. This is general and not about the attributes.

5. [ج1/ص401] speaker: author
   kind: against-ta'wil (batini, only loosely relevant)
   quote: «ولهم من الاصطلاحات التي يريدون لها معان فيما بينهم غير ظواهرها ... فيجعلون الصلاة معرفة أسرارهم، ويريدون بالصوم كتمان أسرارهم»
   note: He condemns the Druze for giving religious terms meanings other than their apparent ones (ظواهر). This is a general stance that the ظاهر should be kept. The context is law and ritual, not the attributes.

6. [ج1/ص411] speaker: author
   kind: explains-meaning (minor)
   quote: «وقوله - تعالى -: ﴿هو أهل التقوى﴾ أي أهل أن يتقى عقابه»
   note: A short gloss on a verse about God while defining التقوى. It is not an attribute-of-essence passage. It is listed only for completeness.

No passages in this part speak of the مفوضة, of بلا كيف, of أمروها كما جاءت, or of what any particular attribute (استواء، يد، نزول, etc.) means.

## Strongest item in this part
Item 1. It speaks of «معاني أسماء الله» as known and believed, with people differing in how well they know them. That presupposes the meaning is affirmed. It is a quotation from Ibn Taymiyya that al-Saffarini endorses, and it is indirect.
