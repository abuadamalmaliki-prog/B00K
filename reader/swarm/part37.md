# Part 37 (ج2/ص220 – ج2/ص236)

Context: this part covers the jinn (creation, food, legal obligation, prophets, marriage), the names of Paradise, who enters it, the present existence and eternity of Paradise and Hell, and the slaughter of death in the form of a ram. No section deals with the divine attributes directly. The items below bear on method only: how he treats ظاهر, تأويل and "consigning the meaning" in non-attribute matters, plus two side remarks about the attributes.

## Evidence
1. [ج2/ص235] speaker: quoting al-Hakim al-Tirmidhi via Mar'i al-Karmi (reported, then effectively set aside: see item 2)
   kind: tafwid-ma'na
   quote: «نقل الحكيم الترمذي أن مذهب السلف في هذا الحديث الوقوف عن الخوض في معناه، فنؤمن به ونكل علمه إلى الله.»
   note: This is one of the answers to "how can death, an accident, come as a ram?" It sets out tafwid of the meaning of the death-ram hadith and calls it the way of the Salaf. It appears in a summary of Mar'i's views ("كل هذا ملخص من كلام الشيخ مرعي"). The subject is a matter of the unseen, not a divine attribute.

2. [ج2/ص236] speaker: author
   kind: against-ta'wil (affirms the apparent meaning, ظاهر)
   quote: «وقال النووي في شرح مسلم: يتأول الحديث على أن الله تعالى يخلق هذا الجسم ثم يذبح مثالا ... قلت: وهذا غير مرضي ولا معول عليه ... قلت: الذي نذهب إليه أن الموت أمر وجودي، وأنه جسم لا عرض، وأنه مخلوق في صورة كبش أملح ... كما صحت بذلك الأخبار»
   note: He rejects al-Nawawi's ta'wil. He also does not stop at the tafwid in item 1: he states a definite positive meaning that follows the apparent text. In this unseen matter he prefers to affirm the meaning over both ta'wil and tafwid.

3. [ج2/ص236] speaker: author
   kind: other (method)
   quote: «على أن كثيرا من العلماء أشار إلى أن جميع المعاني المعقولة عندنا مصورة عند الله تعالى بصور الأجسام ... وإن كنا لا نحس ذلك لكوننا محجوبين عنه، والأحاديث النبوية ناطقة بذلك»
   note: He supports a literal reading of reports about the unseen. Our failure to perceive how something is so does not lead him to deny or reinterpret it. This is a pattern like affirming the meaning while not knowing the how.

4. [ج2/ص224] speaker: author
   kind: defines-terms (ظاهر/تأويل)
   quote: «وتأول الجمهور كل ما ورد من ذلك، ولا يخفى أن ظاهر القرآن مع ما قاله الضحاك والأكثرون على خلافه، وتحقيق ذلك والبحث فيه مما لا فائدة فيه»
   note: On whether the jinn had messengers from among themselves, he notes that the majority used ta'wil against the apparent sense of the Quran. He does not decide the question, saying it has no practical consequence. This shows him using ظاهر and تأويل as opposite terms. Not about the attributes.

5. [ج2/ص225] speaker: author (wording taken from Ibn al-Qayyim, Hadi al-Arwah, without attribution)
   kind: ithbat-ma'na (weak, indirect)
   quote: «اعلم أن للجنة عدة أسماء باعتبار صفاتها، ومسماها واحد باعتبار الذات، فهي مترادفة من هذا الوجه، وهكذا أسماء الرب تعالى»
   note: The divine names are said to work like the names of Paradise: one essence, with each name pointing to a distinct attribute. That implies the names carry distinct meanings.

6. [ج2/ص231] speaker: quoting Ibn al-Qayyim (Hadi al-Arwah) (endorsed; he presents it approvingly as "الإمام المحقق")
   kind: other (against ta'til)
   quote: «وقاسوه سبحانه على خلقه في أفعاله، فهم مشبهة في الأفعال، ودخل التجهم فيهم فصاروا مع ذلك معطلة في الصفات»
   note: Ibn al-Qayyim says the Qadariyya and Mu'tazila liken God to creatures in His acts and, through Jahmi influence, are deniers (معطلة) of the attributes. It is said in passing, while refuting their denial that Paradise and Hell already exist.

## Strongest item in this part
Item 2 (read together with item 1). Al-Saffarini reports the tafwid of the meaning that al-Hakim al-Tirmidhi attributes to the Salaf, rejects al-Nawawi's ta'wil, and then states his own definite reading that follows the apparent text. The subject is a report about the unseen, not a divine attribute, so it is only indirect evidence of his method.
