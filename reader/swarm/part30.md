# Part 30 (ج2/ص96 – ج2/ص113)

Context: this part is entirely eschatology (the descent of Jesus, the Dajjal and Ibn Sayyad, the Jassasa hadith, the start of Gog and Magog). Nothing in it deals directly with the divine attributes. The items below are only side-evidence about his method and terms.

## Evidence
1. [ج2/ص104] speaker: quoting al-Barbahari, Sharh al-Sunna (endorsed: introduced as «من علمائنا» and cited in support of the poem's «خل عن جدال»)
   kind: other (bi-la kayf formula, general)
   quote: «ليس في السنة قياس ولا يضرب لها الأمثال ولا يتبع فيها الأهواء بل هي التصديق بآثار الرسول ﷺ بلا كيف فلا يقال لم؟ ولا كيف؟»
   note: "بلا كيف" here covers the reports in general, not attributes in particular. It rejects asking "why" and "how". It does not say the reports' meaning is unknown, but it does not affirm a meaning either.

2. [ج2/ص103] speaker: author (commenting on the poem's «خل عن جدال»)
   kind: other (method: accept reports, no rational objection)
   quote: «فالأولى التسليم لما ورد على ما ورد والإذعان للأخبار الثابتة فلا تقابل بالمعارضة والرد ... فإنه أمر سمعي أخبر به المعصوم والعقل لا يحيله فوجب اعتقاده والتسليم»
   note: about apparently conflicting reports on Jesus's descent. "التسليم لما ورد على ما ورد" means accepting the report as it stands, but the report's content (what happens, when) is clearly understood. This is acceptance, not consigning the meaning.

3. [ج2/ص106] speaker: author
   kind: other (tanzih: what cannot apply to God)
   quote: «فمعلوم أنه مخلوق يأكل الطعام ويشرب الشراب ثم إنه لخسته وعجزه أعور وهو جسم مرئي وهذه كلها لا تجوز على الباري»
   note: he denies to God being a body (جسم) and being visible (مرئي; the context is this world and the Dajjal's claim to be God). This marks the limit of his affirmation and is useful for weighing how he handles attributes.

4. [ج2/ص105] speaker: author, reporting scholars' views (merely reported)
   kind: defines-terms (تأويل vs ظاهر, مجاز)
   quote: «وقد اختلف العلماء في تأويل هذا الحديث فمنهم من قال هو كناية عن اشتغال الناس ... ومنهم من قال بل هو على ظاهره»; and «وتسمى السنين أياما مجازا»
   note: about the hadith of the Dajjal's long days. Here he uses تأويل for figurative reading (كناية) as opposed to على ظاهره, and accepts مجاز as a valid category in hadith interpretation. No attributes are involved.

## Strongest item in this part
1: the quote from al-Barbahari that he endorses («التصديق بآثار الرسول بلا كيف فلا يقال لم؟ ولا كيف؟»). It is the only formula of the kind the question asks about. It is general, and it denies the "how" without saying the meaning is unknown. This is weak evidence either way.
