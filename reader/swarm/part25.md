# Part 25 (ج2/ص6 – ج2/ص24)

Context: this whole part is about what happens after death (the questioning by Munkar and Nakir, punishment of the grave, the squeezing of the grave, and answers to people who deny it). No passage discusses the divine attributes directly. The items below are indirect or about method only.

## Evidence
1. [ج2/ص17–18] speaker: author (wording close to Ibn al-Qayyim's كتاب الروح, but not marked as a quotation; presented as his own فائدة)
   kind: explains-meaning (weak, indirect)
   quote: «فإن عذاب القبر بل وعذاب الآخرة أثر غضب الله وسخطه على عبده فمن أغضب الله وأسخطه في هذه الدار ... كان له عذاب البرزخ بقدر غضب الله وسخطه عليه»
   note: Treats God's anger and displeasure (الغضب والسخط) as real, as having degrees (بقدر), and as having effects in creation. He keeps anger separate from its effect (the punishment) instead of reading it as "wanting to punish". This gives the attribute positive content.

2. [ج2/ص21] speaker: quoting Ibn al-Qayyim, كتاب الروح (endorsed: introduced as «أئمة الحق ... بما يقمع المفترين»)
   kind: against-ta'wil (method)
   quote: «أن يضم إلى خبر الرسول مراده من غير غلو ولا تقصير ولا يحمل كلامه على ما لا يحتمله ولا يقصر به عن مراده وعما قصده من الهدى والبيان»
   note: A rule for reading revealed reports: take the meaning the speaker intended, without adding to it or cutting it short. This assumes revealed reports carry a meaning that can be known. It is said about the unseen things of the barzakh, not about the attributes.

3. [ج2/ص21] speaker: quoting Ibn al-Qayyim (endorsed)
   kind: other (reason and revelation)
   quote: «الأنبياء لا تأتي بمحالات العقول بل بمحاراتها فكل خبر يظن أن العقل يحيله فلا يخلو من أحد أمرين إما خطأ في النقل أو فساد في العقل»
   note: Rejects the claim that reason rules out what the texts report. This is the usual Hanbali/Taymiyyan argument against ta'wil, applied here to the punishment of the grave.

4. [ج2/ص23] speaker: author (his own summary, «قلت»)
   kind: against-ta'wil (method)
   quote: «ما أخبر به الصادق المصدوق وجب الإيمان به ... ولم تحله العقول وحيث كان ممكنا فمعارضة صحيح الأخبار إلحاد»
   note: In his own voice, calls opposing sound reports with reason "ilhad". The subject is eschatology, not the attributes.

5. [ج2/ص22–23] speaker: quoting Ibn al-Qayyim (endorsed)
   kind: tafwid-kayf-only (analogy only)
   quote: «وسر المسألة أن توسعة القبر وضيقه وإضاءته وخضرته وناره ليس من جنس المعهود في هذا العالم»
   note: Affirms that the reported realities exist and have content, while saying they are not like anything we know in this world. The same pattern as "real meaning, unknown how", but about unseen matters, not attributes.

6. [ج2/ص23] speaker: quoting Ahmad (via Hanbal and al-Marrudhi; endorsed)
   kind: other
   quote: «هذه أحاديث صحاح نؤمن بها ونقر بها، كلما جاء عن النبي ﷺ إسناد جيد أقررنا به»
   note: Ahmad's formula of believing and affirming sound reports, used here for the punishment of the grave.

## Strongest item in this part
1, because it is the only place where a divine attribute (anger and displeasure) gets content: it has degrees and it has effects. Even so, it is incidental and weak. Nothing in this part bears directly on whether the meaning or only the "how" is consigned.
