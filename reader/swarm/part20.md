# Part 20 (ج1/ص366 – ج1/ص381)

## Evidence
This part does not discuss the divine attributes. It covers major and minor sins, the fasiq, the Khawarij/Mu'tazila position on the person who commits major sins, repentance and its acceptance, whether good deeds expiate sins, and the weighing (موازنة) of good and bad deeds. Only two marginal items touch the question, and both are weak:

1. [ج1/ص369] and [ج1/ص379–380] speaker: author (quoting hadith, no commentary)
   kind: other
   quote: «من تقرب مني شبرا تقربت منه ذراعا، ومن تقرب مني ذراعا تقربت منه باعا، ومن آتاني يمشي أتيته هرولة»
   note: He quotes the hadith qudsi on drawing near and هرولة with no ta'wil and no tafwid remark. He uses it only as proof of how wide God's forgiveness is. It is weak evidence: he neither explains these words nor consigns their meaning.

2. [ج1/ص373] speaker: author
   kind: other
   quote: «ويقر ويذعن حسب ما جاء به النبي الكريم وكلام الله القديم»
   note: A passing description of God's speech as قديم, in the section on the apostate's repentance. It does not bear on the meaning-vs-kayfiyya question.

## Strongest item in this part
