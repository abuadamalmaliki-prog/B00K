# Part 16 (ج1/ص291 – ج1/ص308)

This part covers the chapter on human acts (أفعال العباد: created by God, acquired by us) and the تنبيهات on the Qadariyya and the Jabriyya. Nothing in it is explicitly about تفويض or إثبات معنى الصفات. The items below are the few passages that touch the divine attributes indirectly.

## Evidence
1. [ج1/ص295] speaker: author (his own explanation of the verse ((مراد لربنا)), using wording close to Ibn Taymiyya's)
   kind: explains-meaning
   quote: «وهو - تعالى - يحب المحسنين، والمتقين، ويرضى عن السابقين الأولين ... ولا يحب الفساد، ولا يرضى لعباده الكفر»
   note: He separates God's will (إرادة/مشيئة) from His love and pleasure (المحبة/الرضا) and treats love and pleasure as real attributes with their own content. They are not reduced to will.

2. [ج1/ص300] speaker: author (continuing the Ibn Taymiyya-style passage on الفرقة الأولى of the Qadariyya)
   kind: explains-meaning
   quote: «وهو يخلق بمشيئته، فهو يعلمه ويريده، وإرادته - تعالى - قائمة بنفسه، وقد يتكلم به ويخبر به»
   note: Will is affirmed as an attribute that subsists in God's self (قائمة بنفسه), and speech as something God may speak and announce. Both are given positive content.

3. [ج1/ص307] speaker: quoting Ibn Taymiyya (endorsed; the author quotes him at length with approval)
   kind: explains-meaning / other
   quote: «ولا سيما إذا قرنوا به توحيد أهل الكلام المثبتين للقدر والمشيئة من غير إثبات المحبة والبغض والرضا والسخط، الذين يقولون: التوحيد هو توحيد الربوبية، وأما الإلهية فهي عندهم القدرة على الاختراع»
   note: He criticises the mutakallimun for leaving out love, hatred, pleasure and anger. This assumes those attributes must be affirmed as distinct realities.

4. [ج1/ص302] speaker: quoting Ibn Taymiyya (endorsed)
   kind: against-ta'wil (in the broad sense of negating attributes)
   quote: «لأنهم سلكوا في ردهم عليهم مسلك جهم بن صفوان وأتباعه، فنفوا حكمة الله في خلقه وأمره، ونفوا رحمته بعباده»
   note: Denying God's wisdom and mercy is condemned as following Jahm. This implies that wisdom and mercy are affirmed as real attributes.

5. [ج1/ص292] speaker: quoting al-Qadi Abu Ya'la (reported; he lists it among definitions of القدرة)
   kind: other
   quote: «وقال القاضي الإمام، من علمائنا: خلق الشيء بقوله " كن " وهو قائم بالله غير بائن منه ومراده»
   note: A Hanbali formula: creating by the word «كن», which subsists in God and is not separate from Him. It concerns speech and creation as attributes, but it comes up only in passing.

6. [ج1/ص303–304] speaker: quoting hadith and athar (reported)
   kind: other (do not read this as tafwid of the attributes)
   quote: «القدر سر الله» ... «سر الله خفي عليك فلا تفشه»
   note: Here the "secret" that is withheld is qadar, not the meaning of an attribute. It may be cited elsewhere as a parallel, so it is noted for completeness.

7. [ج1/ص293] speaker: quoting al-Taftazani (reported, on كسب)
   kind: defines-terms (marginal)
   quote: «وهذا القدر من المعنى الضروري، وإن لم نقدر على أزيد من ذلك في تلخيص العبارة»
   note: The admission that one cannot explain further is about the doctrine of كسب, not about the divine attributes. It is recorded only so nobody misreads it.

## Strongest item in this part
Item 3 (backed by items 1 and 2). The endorsed Ibn Taymiyya quote treats love, hatred, pleasure and anger as attributes that must be affirmed as distinct from will and power. That only makes sense if their meaning is affirmed. Still, it is indirect evidence, because this part is about qadar.

## Coverage
Read lines 1–118 of 118. Lines 1–5 (ص290) and 115–118 (ص309) are the overlap and were not used as evidence.
