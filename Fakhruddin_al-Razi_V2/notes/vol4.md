## L13144– Part 1 of vol. 4: "inquiries of those who hold eternity"; Maqāla 1, preliminaries (six muqaddimas, per MS S)
MS S opening: "Book four of the theology of *al-Maṭālib al-ʿĀliya*, on origination and eternity and the secrets of time and eternity; in it
are maqālas: maqāla 1 on the preliminaries, which are six."
### Muqaddima 1: what "the world" (ʿālam) means (L13154–)
- Existents are spatial (mutaḥayyiz), inhering in the spatial, or neither. Spatial: divisible (body) or not (**the indivisible part (al-juzʾ
  alladhī lā yatajazzaʾ), "for those who affirm it"**). Inhering: accidents, which for the sages (ḥukamāʾ) fall into nine genera.
- **Quality (kayf)** has two kinds: accidents possible in non-living things and those only in the living. The first: (a) sensibles perceived
  primarily by one of the five senses: colours and lights (sight), letters and sounds (hearing), **the nine tastes**, smells, tangibles (hot,
  cold, wet, dry and what arises from their combination); (b) **akwān** ("being-in-place"; *ḥayyiz* to be explained in the bāb on the void
  and direction), a genus of four: **motion = first occurrence in a second place; rest = second occurrence in a first place; junction
  (ijtimāʿ) = two spatial things in two places with no void between; separation (iftirāq) = with a void between.**
- Accidents only in the living: life, plus attributes conditioned on life, for perception or for action. *Perception*: (1) beliefs:
  conceptions or assents; assents firm or not; firm ones true or not; true ones necessary, speculative (naẓarī) or imitative (taqlīdī).
  Necessary ones are from the five senses or primary (conceiving subject and predicate suffices); taqlīd = firm belief without proof or
  doubt; firm untrue = compound ignorance (jahl murakkab); non-firm: equal = doubt (shakk), preponderant = opinion (ẓann), outweighed = fancy
  (wahm). (2) sense perceptions. (3) reflection (naẓar, fikr): disputed whether a kind of belief or distinct. (4) pain and pleasure:
  **disputed whether pain is the very perception of the incompatible (idrāk al-munāfī) or an additional state.** *Action*: power; will and
  dislike; appetite and aversion (disputed whether these are will and dislike or distinct genera). "A brief pointer to the kinds of accidents."
